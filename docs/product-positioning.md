# Talqyla product position

## One-line promise

Talqyla is a Russian/Kazakh-ready debate coach for school students: a 10-minute structured sparring round that turns one weak reasoning skill into the next practice plan.

## Who it is for

- Primary user: student in grades 7–11 who needs daily debate reps without a partner.
- Buyer/influencer: parent, debate coach, olympiad center, or school program.
- First wedge: Russian-speaking school debate clubs and tournament preparation in Kazakhstan and nearby markets.

## What we refuse to be

- Not a general-purpose chat assistant.
- Not a multi-model “which AI is right?” spectacle.
- Not a fake realtime voice call while the backend is turn-based.
- Not a generic speech score with no next action.

## The product wedge

### Structured round, not chat

Every session has a visible contract: build Claim → Warrant → Impact, face a targeted cross-question, answer with a counterpunch, then receive a ballot.

### Local tournament literacy

The next product layer should support recognizable school formats and language: World Schools-style proposition/opposition, short crossfire, weighing, POI-like interruptions later, and Russian/Kazakh debate vocabulary. Start with one reliable format before adding format sprawl.

### Longitudinal skill graph

The system should remember only what helps learning: scores by skill, recurring failure mode, last successful correction, and the next drill. The point is not “AI memory”; it is visible progress a student, coach, or parent can understand.

### Coach-grade ballot

Every result needs four things: what landed, what failed, an exact quote or moment, and one drill for the next round. Never ship generic praise as feedback.

## Product loop

1. Choose a motion and a skill focus.
2. Build a compact case.
3. Debate three meaningful exchanges.
4. Get a ballot with evidence.
5. Repeat the weakest move in a shorter drill.
6. See whether the same skill improves over time.

## North-star metrics

- First-round completion rate.
- Second-round start rate after the ballot.
- Improvement on the focused skill after three rounds.
- Percentage of feedback items containing a usable quote and a concrete drill.
- Coach/parent agreement with the judge on reviewed golden-set rounds.

## Roadmap discipline

Before adding a feature, ask: does it make the next debate rep more realistic, more educational, or more measurable? If not, it is probably demo garnish.
